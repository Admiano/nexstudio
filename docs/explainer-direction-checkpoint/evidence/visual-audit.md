# Lightning and thunder: development film audit

## Verdict

Real videos were generated by the independent explainer pipeline, but the visual result fails the requested standard. Successful encoding and schema audits are not creative-quality acceptance.

Each file contains H264 video and AAC audio. All 1,526 artifacts declared in the production manifest were checked against their recorded sizes and SHA256 hashes with no mismatch. Code verification after the compiler fix: 388 tests passed, 1,292 subtests passed, Ruff passed, and mypy passed for all nine package source files. There is no repository pre-commit configuration. Two test warnings explicitly deny production creative authority.

## Source and authoring validity

Evidence: https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-science-thunder

Narration preserves the four supplied NOAA sentences unchanged. This is an explicitly selected source-quotation development mode, not a demonstration of general original narration authoring.

The meaning graph, visual direction and motion choices came from local model calls. The final run reused unchanged recorded live responses from the preceding attempt after fixing the compiler. No fixture, manually authored score or replacement scene was used. Liam audio was produced by Kokoro and choreography was bound to provider word intervals.

This does not certify the diagrams as factually clear. In particular, the on-screen label “sound wave from thunder” is misleading: the source describes a sound wave heard as thunder, not thunder generating a separate sound wave.

## Visual and temporal findings

All three encoded films were inspected with video analysis, representative rendered frames, and the native plans and property tracks.

- The opening is visually empty while narration has already begun. The first directed action begins around 1.84 seconds, and the first shape construction around 2.29 seconds.
- View changes move multiple actors into the same shared focus position. Shapes overlap and obscure one another, and causal links collapse instead of preserving clear relationships.
- Labels are small and gathered in a separate bottom caption band rather than visibly attached to their corresponding actors. Labels can appear before the associated shape has been constructed.
- Expansion and contraction are named, but not demonstrated through explanatory physical state changes. The film mainly constructs generic circles, rectangles, a diamond and a hexagon.
- The combined expansion-and-contraction process remains present in the score, but a generic hexagon does not explain how the joint process creates the sound wave.
- Some attention/view actions occur before construction. Changing opacity or highlight does not ensure visible change when a shape still has zero draw and fill.
- The first scene fades out over 350 ms before the second scene. There is no meaningful visual continuity between the two process views.
- The audio is intelligible and the score uses measured word cues, but synchronized cue selection alone does not make the animation explanatory.
- The sound ledger contains no additional directed sound bindings. This sample has narration, not a developed sound-design layer.

## Remaining proof

The compact route can reach actual rendering, but its visual proof is not accepted. The next investigation should address visible-state prerequisites, collision-safe movement, label attachment, source-correct display language, and explanatory state-change capabilities. More unconstrained model retries or topic-specific scene patches would not resolve those boundaries.

Authority remains DEVELOPMENT_PREFLIGHT. There is no authenticated P8 approval, Creative Lock, human calibration, public dispatch, or production certification.
