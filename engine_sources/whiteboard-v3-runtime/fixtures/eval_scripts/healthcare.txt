# How a Vaccine Reaches You
## The Trial
A researcher tests the vaccine in the lab. The clinician enrolls a patient in the trial.

## Approval
The regulator reviews the data. The doctor approves the treatment.

## The Clinic
The nurse administers the vaccine to the patient. The pharmacist stores the vial in the fridge.

## Recovery
The immune cell attacks the virus. The doctor monitors the heart on the monitor.
