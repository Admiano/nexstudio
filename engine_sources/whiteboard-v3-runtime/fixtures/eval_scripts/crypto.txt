# How Tokenized Assets Work
## The Vault
A custodian holds the real asset in a vault. The auditor verifies the reserve.

## Minting
The developer mints a token on the blockchain. The smart contract records the hash.

## Trading
An investor buys a fractional share. The trader exchanges the token for money.

## The Attack
A scammer tries to steal the private key. The wallet locks the vault and alerts the network.
