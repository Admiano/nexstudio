# Managing a Forest Fire
## Dry Season
A long drought leaves the pine forest dry and full of fallen logs.

## The Spark
One campfire sends sparks into the grass, and smoke rises over the trees.

## Firefighters Respond
A firefighter uses a hose while a helicopter drops water on the flames.

## Regrowth
Before the fire the forest was crowded, but now new seedlings grow in the sunlight.
