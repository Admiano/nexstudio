# How Wolves Changed a River
## The Missing Wolves
Without wolves, the deer grazed the riverbanks bare and the trees disappeared.

## The Return
Then rangers brought the wolves back, and the deer moved away from the river.

## Trees Recover
The willow trees grew again, and birds returned to nest in the branches.

## A Healthier River
Now beavers build dams in the river, and the fish population is rising.
