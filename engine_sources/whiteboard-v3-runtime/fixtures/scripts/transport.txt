# How a City Fixed Traffic
## Gridlock
Every morning the cars crawl through the city, and the driver feels stressed.

## A New Bus Lane
The city painted a bus lane, so the bus moves past the traffic jam.

## Bikes Join In
Commuters ride a bicycle on the bridge, and the air gets cleaner.

## Results
Before the change commutes took an hour, but now the train and bus arrive on time.
