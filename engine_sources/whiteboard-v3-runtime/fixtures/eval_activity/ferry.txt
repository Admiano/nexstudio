# The Morning Ferry
## Boarding
The sailor ties the rope to the dock. A driver drives his car onto the ferry. A woman walks up the ramp with a suitcase.

## Crossing
The captain steers the ferry across the bay. A boy watches the gulls from the deck. His grandmother pours coffee from a flask into a cup.

## Arrival
The sailor throws the rope to a worker on the pier. The driver drives off the ferry. The boy runs toward his uncle.
