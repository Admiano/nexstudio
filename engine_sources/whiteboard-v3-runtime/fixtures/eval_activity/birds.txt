# The Bird Watchers
## The Hide
The guide leads the group to a wooden hut. The old man watches the pond from the hut. The woman looks at a heron through her binoculars.

## Feeding
The girl throws bread into the water. A duck swims toward the girl. The guide fills the feeder with seeds.

## Notes
The woman writes the bird names in a notebook. The old man photographs the heron with a camera. The girl drinks tea from a flask.
