# A Night at the Lake
## Setting Up
The father pulls the tent from the car. The daughter hammers the pegs into the ground with a rock. The mother carries firewood to the fire pit.

## Supper
The father pours water from a bottle into the pot. The daughter stirs the soup with a spoon. The mother hands a bowl to the daughter.

## Stars
The daughter looks at the stars through a telescope. The father points at the moon. The mother reads a book by the lantern.
