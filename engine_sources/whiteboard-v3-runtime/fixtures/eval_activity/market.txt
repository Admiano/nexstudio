# Saturday Market
## Opening
The farmer lifts crates of apples onto the table. His son hangs a sign on the tent. A baker slides bread into a basket.

## Customers
A woman fills her bag with oranges. The farmer weighs the apples on a scale. A boy gives a coin to the baker.

## Closing
The son sweeps the floor with a broom. The farmer loads the empty crates into the truck. The baker waves from the van.
