# Sunday Pancakes
## Batter
The grandfather cracks eggs into a bowl. The girl pours milk into the bowl. The grandfather mixes the batter with a whisk.

## Cooking
The grandfather pours batter into the pan. The girl flips a pancake with a spatula. The dog watches from the doorway.

## Breakfast
The girl carries the plates to the table. The grandfather pours syrup onto the pancakes. The family eats at the table.
