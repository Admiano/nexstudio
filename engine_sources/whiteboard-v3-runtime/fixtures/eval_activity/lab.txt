# The Science Class
## The Experiment
The teacher pours blue liquid into a flask. A student watches the flask from her desk. Another student writes the result in a notebook.

## The Microscope
The girl looks through the microscope at a leaf. The boy draws the leaf on the board with chalk. The teacher places a slide under the lens.

## Cleanup
The students wash the beakers in the sink. The teacher wipes the table with a cloth. The girl puts the notebook into her bag.
