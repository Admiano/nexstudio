# Moving Day at the Office
## Packing
The manager packs files into a box. An intern tapes the box with tape. A mover lifts the desk onto a trolley.

## The Van
The mover pushes the trolley into the van. The driver drives the van to the new building. The intern looks out the window at the city.

## Unpacking
The manager places a plant on the shelf. The intern hangs a clock on the wall. The mover sits on a box and drinks water from a bottle.
