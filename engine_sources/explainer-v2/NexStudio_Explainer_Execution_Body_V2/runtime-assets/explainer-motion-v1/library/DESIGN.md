# NexStudio Paper Motion — Design System

## Style Prompt
A premium modern editorial paper-motion system with tactile grain, layered cut-paper depth, natural shadows, printed ink, restrained handmade imperfection, and clean cinematic composition. It must feel crafted but never childish, rustic, glossy, plastic, or photorealistically 3D.

## Presets
- `clean-editorial`: quiet white stock, precise edges, restrained grain, soft lift.
- `handmade-scrapbook`: warm fibres, torn edges, tape, visible layering, lively but controlled motion.
- `technical-notebook`: graph/notebook structures, ink lines, annotations, measured motion.
- `bold-paper-collage`: strong cut shapes, larger shadows, energetic overlap, high-contrast print.

## Core Theme Tokens
`--paper-bg`, `--paper-surface`, `--paper-surface-alt`, `--ink`, `--ink-muted`, `--primary`, `--secondary`, `--accent`, `--highlight`, `--shadow-color`, `--shadow-opacity`, `--grain-opacity`, `--outline-width`.

## Typography
- Display: Arial Black, Helvetica Neue, sans-serif.
- Body: Inter, Arial, sans-serif.
- Technical labels: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace.
- Render minimums: 60px headline, 20px body, 16px data label at 1920×1080.

## Depth
Use three logical planes: surface, attached object, lifted object. Shadows must suggest paper thickness rather than floating glass.

## Motion
Paper has weight. Entrances settle; folds rotate around believable hinges; grain moves almost imperceptibly. Motion must be deterministic and seekable.

## What Not To Do
- No glossy 3D plastic icons.
- No childish classroom-craft styling.
- No full-screen dark linear gradients that band in H.264.
- No random jitter or unseeded procedural movement.
- No flattened animation used as source.
