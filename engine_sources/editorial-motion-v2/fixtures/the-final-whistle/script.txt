On the last night of the season, a whole town held its breath.
The captain walked out under the lights, alone, boots laced tight.
Then the rain came down like static, and still nobody left.
The ball moved faster than the weather.
One touch, one strike, and the net billowed like a sail.
The cup rose under the floodlights, and the town rose with it.
Some nights end. This one never did.
